01 — the conceptin her words

the concept

memory box is a six-song project about archiving what "home" feels like. Not just as a place, but as the feeling of being grounded.

Each song is an old photo pulled from the bottom of your unorganized drawer that unlocks a flood of nostalgia.

Over the past four years, I've lived through moments that shook my sense of self such as moving between homes, rebuilding communities from scratch, losing myself in one person, feeling trapped in a controlling environment. Each of these was its own specific time and place, and each one felt like "home" to me, if only for a while.

“memory box” is what happens when you lay all those vignettes out together in one place, in one box, so you can trace the pieces that made you who you are.

— hunjiya / 현지아
